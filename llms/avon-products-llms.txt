# Avon Products

> Avon Products (Avon International) is a 140-year-old direct-selling beauty company — cosmetics,
> skincare, fragrance and personal care sold through millions of independent Representatives in
> markets outside North America. Founded in 1886 in New York, it was a Fortune 500 company until
> Natura &Co acquired it in January 2020; Regent acquired Avon International from Natura for a
> nominal £1 plus earn-outs in January 2026 and, in August 2026, agreed to buy Avon North America
> (The Avon Company, avon.com) from LG Household & Health Care to reunite the two businesses. The
> international company is headquartered in Northampton, UK and its corporate site is avonworldwide.com.

**Avon Products publishes no public API.** As of 2026-09-18 there is no developer portal, no API
reference, no SDK, no webhook catalog, no MCP server, no agent card and no machine-readable
contract of any kind — no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Postman collection, apis.json or
llms.txt — on any host this company controls. The historical corporate domain `avon-products.com`
is a GoDaddy for-sale parking page that answers 200 with the same HTML shell for every path; the
former `developer.avon.com` host no longer resolves in DNS; and `www.avonworldwide.com` is a
corporate brand site (about-us, careers, responsible-business, news) served behind an Akamai edge
policy that returns 403 to unauthenticated crawlers.

A Springfox Swagger 2.0 surface does exist at `api.avon.com` (five groups: Avon.com, AvonU,
CareCenter, DigitalCatalog, Ivr), but `www.avon.com` identifies itself as **The Avon Company**
(Avon North America, New York, "along with LG Household and Healthcare") — a company that has been
separate from Avon Products since 2016 — so that contract is not recorded on this profile. It is
an internal e-commerce/IVR backend, not a published developer product. Do not call `api.avon.com`
on the strength of this profile.

This file is generated by API Evangelist from a public profile of Avon Products. It is not
published by Avon Products.
